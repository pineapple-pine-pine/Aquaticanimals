Ocean Depth Zones

Sunlight Zone

0 – 200 metres

Plenty of sunlight and marine life.

Twilight Zone

200 – 1,000 metres

Very little sunlight reaches this region.

Midnight Zone

1,000 – 4,000 metres

Completely dark and extremely cold.

🕳️ Abyssal Zone

4,000 – 6,000 metres

Extreme pressure and almost no food.

🐠 Animals by Depth

0 – 200 m

Dolphin

Intelligent marine mammals commonly found in shallow and coastal waters.

0 – 200 m

Sea Turtle

Ancient reptiles that spend much of their lives in ocean environments.

200 – 1,000 m

Manta Ray

Large filter-feeding rays that can travel through different ocean zones.

200 – 1,000 m

Swordfish

Fast predators capable of diving into deeper waters to hunt prey.

1,000 – 4,000 m

Anglerfish

A deep-sea predator famous for its glowing lure used to attract prey.

1,000 – 4,000 m

Giant Squid

One of the largest invertebrates on Earth, living deep beneath the surface.

4,000 – 6,000 m

Deep-Sea Crab

Adapted to extreme pressure and cold conditions on the ocean floor.

Explore Beneath the Surface

The ocean is divided into several depth zones, each with unique environmental conditions and fascinating animals.

Light

Sunlight decreases rapidly as depth increases.

❄️ Temperature

Deeper waters are generally colder.

Pressure

Pressure increases dramatically with depth.
